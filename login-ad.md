# What's New: August 2026 Release Notes### New Features* **TrackAbout Web » Orders » Cancel Order Record**: When an order is canceled in TrackAbout, a new **Cancel Order** record is now created, and an outbound integration message is sent when that record is verified. This feature is off by default. Contact TrackAbout Support to enable it for your account.* **TrackAbout Mobile » Scan Assets » Maintenance on Containers**: TrackAbout Mobile now supports scanning and performing maintenance on containers, provided the container is empty.  * You can scan a container barcode in a maintenance action to perform maintenance on the container itself.  * If the container is empty, maintenance proceeds and a record is created for that container.  * If the container has assets inside, the scan is rejected: "Container must be empty to perform maintenance."* **TrackAbout Web » Customer Portal**: The Customer Portal has been significantly expanded so your customers can see assets they own that are currently being held or serviced at your facility, view their full inventory, and track their maintenance orders from start to finish. The Portal includes these new features.  * **Expanded Asset Visibility**: Customers can now see assets they own that are in-house at your facility alongside their delivered assets.  * **In-House Asset History Page**: Lets your customers view details of their assets as well as any historical transactions you choose to allow them to view.  * **Portal Inventory Page**: Gives your customers a full picture of their assets: those at their own locations and those currently in for service at your facility.  * **Maintenance Order Search**: Lets your customers find and track maintenance orders for their assets.  * **Maintenance Order Details Page**: Gives your customer a complete view of a single maintenance work order, including its overall status and a line-by-line breakdown of every service that was ordered and how many assets have been serviced so far.  * **Serviced Asset Details View**: Gives your customers a detailed, per-asset view of the work completed on a maintenance order service line.* **TrackAbout Web » Maintenance » Collisions**: TrackAbout now uses the following AI features to help resolve duplicate-barcode collisions. Note that both features are turned off by default, and must be enabled by TrackAbout Support.  * The **Resolve collision** page now includes an AI **Suggestion Panel** to help you resolve duplicate-barcode collisions faster and more consistently. When you open a collision, the panel analyzes the candidates in the background and returns a recommended action, a confidence indicator, and a summary explaining why the recommendation was made. You can also rate the quality of AI-generated resolution suggestions (optional). Rating the suggestions helps improve future suggestions.  * The **Resolve collision** page can now resolve high-confidence collisions automatically in the background, without requiring you to act on each one. When a new collision is created, TrackAbout evaluates it in the background. If the AI recommendation meets or exceeds your configured confidence threshold, the collision is resolved automatically and leaves the queue. Lower-confidence collisions and anything the AI flags for manual review stay in the queue.* **Important**: At the same time as this release, we will be clearing out old collision records, so your team isn't sorting through stale collisions while trying to resolve current ones. Any collision on a record older than July 1, 2026, will be deleted as part of this release. If you'd like to keep your existing collision history and opt out of this cleanup, please contact TrackAbout Support (**ta-support@datacor.com**).### Enhancements* **TrackAbout Web Integration Messages**: Change of Service integration messages now include an **In Maintenance** indicator so that your integration system can distinguish a maintenance-driven change of service from a routine one.* **TrackAbout Mobile » Paused Deliveries**: You can now save a delivery after resuming it from a paused state without being required to add new assets or line items.* **TrackAbout Web » OpenData**: You can now access rack and asset family relationship data directly through TrackAbout's OpenData views. Previously, this information was only available through internal systems, making it difficult to include in custom reports or data exports. With this addition, you can see which asset families are associated with each rack in your facility and combine that information with other asset data to get a more complete picture for reporting and analysis.* **TrackAbout Web » Asset History**: Asset History now displays all subactions for a maintenance record, sorted by sort order and then by name. If the full list exceeds the display limit, a **more...** indicator appears; hovering over it shows a tooltip with the complete list of subactions.### Bug Fixes* **TrackAbout Mobile » Delivery Action**: We resolved an issue where tapping **Back** on the Enter Assets screen and then switching to a different order caused previously entered assets to carry over, resulting in incorrect line item numbers on the saved delivery. Tapping **Back** now prompts for confirmation before canceling the delivery and clearing all entered items. A new Order Details screen also lets drivers review order and customer information without leaving the Enter Assets screen.
